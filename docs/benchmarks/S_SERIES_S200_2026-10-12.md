## Claim boundary, method and next work

- Each counted 200-node cohort used 200 independent identity-bearing **runtime processes**, a run-isolated **loopback relay**, real model calls and managed cloud provider paths. These data do **not** establish 200 geographically distributed physical machines, a physical WAN relay, production SLO/mainnet, or deployment to 200 independent sites.
- **Six families were actually measured:** GPT, Gemini, Grok, Llama, Mistral, Kimi. **DeepSeek was excluded** by the frozen execution configuration. The original public contract's **seven-family** requirement remains outstanding; never silently redefine it as six or use the six-family PASS as seven-family qualification.
- The mixed-SHA proof shows **composite correctness under immutable preserved scenario evidence**, **not** one homogeneous exact-SHA, simultaneously rerun suite. Repeating a passing stage would lose the incremental method's cost controls. Any claim specifically requiring a homogeneous exact-SHA or simultaneous multi-scenario proof remains a different future test.
- Improvements: fix semantic NEED→ACK tail-latency, dispatch and queueing; validate cost-optimal selection independently; qualify seven-provider parity; execute externally distributed WAN/SLO and subsequent S-500 as distinct frozen campaigns.
- Historical A14 failure and all prior attempts are retained under append-only/redact-not-delete policy. Private raw traces, private provider/cloud topology, credentials, quotas and secrets are not copied to public docs.
