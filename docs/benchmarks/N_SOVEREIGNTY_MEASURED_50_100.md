# N/SOVEREIGNTY measured 50/100 contract

The accepted 10-node qualification is preserved unchanged. SOVEREIGNTY-50 first copies its safety semantics at 50-node scale, then adds real measurement. SOVEREIGNTY-100 scales the accepted measured 50-node harness without weakening those semantics.

A measured cell is valid only with unique real TRUYN node identities/endpoints across Azure and GCP and at least three jurisdiction classes. Synthetic fixture latency/cost cannot satisfy measured acceptance.

Every measured cell records end-to-end RTT p50/p95/p99, throughput, success rate, routing-decision p50/p95/p99, paired unrestricted/restricted sovereignty overhead, node-runtime cost, data-egress cost/bytes, and proof that forbidden and impossible-policy flows never executed.

Each cell remains independently immutable, exact-SHA admitted, coordinator-gated and separately reconciled. A 10-node or 50-node GREEN never substitutes for the next scale.
