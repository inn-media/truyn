## Interpretation and boundary

S-50 demonstrates a bounded 50-node heterogeneous semantic intelligence network across seven provider families, two clouds, three regions and two geographies, including multi-hop provenance, policy-aware routing, concurrent load, multilingual retrieval and recovery from node/provider/network faults.

It does **not** establish universal throughput, pricing, latency, geographic coverage or production-mainnet capacity. S-100, S-200 and S-500 remain independent gates.

The main scale-performance issue exposed by S-50 is contention latency/backpressure. The next S-Series acceptance level is **S-100**.
