## Interpretation and boundary

S-100 demonstrates a bounded 100-node heterogeneous semantic intelligence network across seven provider families, two clouds, three regions and two geographies, including multi-hop provenance, policy-aware routing, concurrent load, multilingual retrieval and recovery from node/provider/network faults.

It does **not** establish universal throughput, universal latency, universal pricing, arbitrary geographic coverage, production-mainnet capacity, or S-200/S-500 acceptance.

The primary engineering question before S-200 is no longer the eliminated Azure auth stampede. It is the measured **4.586 s p50 / 5.874 s p95 non-provider residual under 100-way contention**, which should be decomposed into requester->relay, relay->worker, worker scheduling, result submission and requester receive stages.

The next independent semantic-scale acceptance gate is **S-200**.
